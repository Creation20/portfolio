MeetActions

Turn meetings into decisions and to-dos

An AI meeting assistant. Record the meeting or upload a recording, take photos of the whiteboard, and it gives you a written record, the decisions made and a to-do list showing who does what. It pauses recording by itself when the room goes quiet.

  • Listens and looks: understands both the audio and whiteboard photos
  • Auto-pause when nobody is speaking
  • One-tap bookmarks for important moments and decisions
  • Private by design: each team only sees its own meetings

  • Express
  • MongoDB

AI that reads the mood of a conversation

An app that records a conversation on camera and lets AI study it. You get a clear report on signs of stress in the voice and face, hesitation and defensive language, with a written record, a score out of 100 and a PDF you can share.   • Studies voice, face and words together

How we'll work

You don't need to know anything technical. Tell me what your business needs, and I'll guide you from first idea to a finished product your customers love using.

Step 1

We talk.

A free call about your business, your customers and what's slowing you down. I listen first, then suggest what will actually help, even if that's something small.

Step 2

You see it before it's built.

I design how your website or app will look and work, and you give feedback. You get a clear price and timeline up front, so there are no surprises later.

Step 3

I build, you watch it grow.

You get a link to try it as it comes together, with a short update every week. You can change your mind along the way without starting over.

Step 4

Launch, and I stay with you.

I get it live, show you and your team how to use it, and stay on hand to fix anything and help it grow as your business does.
